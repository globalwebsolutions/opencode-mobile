سياسة الخصوصية — OpenCode Mobile

OpenCode Mobile تطبيق غير رسمي بيوصّل موبايلك بسيرفر OpenCode اللي انت مشغّله على جهازك. مفيش عندنا سيرفرات، ولا حسابات، ولا تتبّع، ولا إعلانات، وإحنا مش بنجمع أي بيانات عنك.

البيانات اللي بتتخزن على جهازك

  • عنوان سيرفر OpenCode بتاعك + توكن التوصيل (pairing token) — في تخزين مشفّر على الجهاز (Android Keystore على أندرويد، Keychain على iOS). التوكن ده بيتولد وقت ما انت بتعمل pairing، وبيستخدم للمصادقة مع سيرفرك انت بس.
  • تفضيلات التطبيق: اللغة والمظهر.
  • إعدادات الإشعارات: عنوان وتوكن السيرفر — عشان خدمة الخلفية تقراها.
  • الصور المرفقة: بتتبعت لسيرفرك وتُخزَّن عنده.

مفيش أي بيانات بتترسل لـ Global Web Solutions. إحنا مش بنشوف سيرفرك ولا محتواه.

Privacy Policy — OpenCode Mobile

OpenCode Mobile is an unofficial client that connects your phone to the OpenCode server you run on your own computer. There are no servers of ours, no accounts, no tracking and no ads, and we do not collect any data about you.

Stored on your device

  • Your OpenCode server address and its pairing token, kept in encrypted storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain). The token authorises requests to your server only.
  • App preferences (language, theme).
  • Notification settings: your server address and token, so the background service can read them.
  • Attached images: uploaded to your server and stored there.

Nothing is sent to Global Web Solutions. We cannot see your server or its contents.

Leaving the device

DestinationWhatWhen
Your OpenCode server (on your network) your messages, permission replies, run commands, attached images while you use the app
opencode.ai (optional) the OpenCode account token held by your server — to read your plan quota only only when you open the quota card — never conversations

Traffic to your server uses plain HTTP on your local network, exactly as the OpenCode server itself works. Use Tailscale for full encryption.

We do not use

  • Any analytics, tracking or advertising service.
  • Any account of ours — no sign-up, no email, no phone number.

Children

A developer tool, not aimed at children (18+). We collect nothing from anyone.

Deleting your data

  • In the app: Settings → delete server (removes the address and token), or clear the app's data in system settings.
  • On your computer: conversations live on your own OpenCode server — delete them there.
  • We hold no data to delete.
